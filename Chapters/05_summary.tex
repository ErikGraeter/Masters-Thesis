\chapter{Summary of forecasts}
\label{chap:results_summary}

This chapter consolidates the sensitivity studies of
\Cref{chap:steam_sensitivity} and \Cref{chap:parameter_sensitivity}
into a single comparison of forecast scenarios, identifying which
combinations of physical assumptions place the \HZIED detection
into the moderate, strong, or decisive evidence regime for the
\PLATO P4 and P5 samples.  Figures in this chapter are produced by
\texttt{Src/figures/ch10\_summary.py}.
