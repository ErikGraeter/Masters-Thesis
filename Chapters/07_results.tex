\section{Planet yield across the PLATO samples}
\Cref{fig:plato_sample_counts} shows the simulated detectable-planet yield across the five \PLATO samples on the radus-instellation plane. While P3 was removed form PLATO mission due to historical reasons, P4 and P5 (M-dwarfs and faint-FGK dwarfs) provide a sufficient number of detectable rocky planets for the HZIED analysis. In contrast, the P4 M-dwarf sample and the P5 faint-FGK sample each provide a sufficient number of planets that could enter the analysis.

\section{The \textcite{Schlecker2024} fiducial as a baseline for PLATO}
\label{sec:reproduction}
The first PLATO specific test replicates the \textcite{Schlecker2024}
fiducial model on the real target populations. We generate again a population of planets in the radius--instellation plane, and inject a radius inflation at $S_\mathrm{thresh} = 280 W m^{-2}$ according to the \textcite{Turbet2020} steam
mass--radius grid for a fraction of 0.8 of all planets,and apply a \textcite{DornLichtenberg2021} water-retention correction (DL-correction). The water-to-rock ratio is set to 0.005. First, the tabulation of
\textcite{Turbet2020} covers only rocky planet masses between $M \in [0.1,\,2.05]\,\Mearth$; above $2.05\,\Mearth$ the model
provides no steam-atmosphere radius, so the observable cuts of restrict the analysis to that same range and
any hot-side planet heavier than $2.05\,\Mearth$ is dropped from the
sample. This mass ceiling makes the comparison directly reproducible with \textcite{Schlecker2024}, since the same population of planets is generated, but with exchanged stellar catalogue. \Cref{fig:schlecker_on_plato} shows the resulting
radius--instellation distributions with the SMA band and posterior
draws overlaid.


\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{schlecker_on_plato}
    \caption[Schlecker+2024 fiducial on PLATO P4 and P5]{%

        applied to the real \PLATO P4 and P5 target samples. The
