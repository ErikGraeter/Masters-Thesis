\chapter{Model Setup and Validation}
\label{chap:setup_validation}

Before presenting the scientific results, this chapter documents the specific
configuration used for the analysis and demonstrates that the extended
pipeline recovers known results under controlled conditions.  This provides
a trust foundation for the extensions presented in \Cref{chap:results}.
The fiducial simulation configuration is documented in
\Cref{sec:simulation_config} (\Cref{chap:methodology}); the loading and
diagnostic checks on the \PLATO Input Catalogue are documented in
\Cref{sec:pic_integration} (\Cref{chap:plato_mission}).  This chapter
covers only the validation checks that are not natural fits in the
methodology or mission chapters.


\section{Reproduction of the Schlecker+2024 Baseline}
\label{sec:schlecker_reproduction}

\emph{Scaffold.}
Reproduction of the headline detectability result of \textcite{Schlecker2024}
on a generic Gaia-based sample. Comparison of the recovered
$\Delta \ln Z$ and posterior on $S_{\mathrm{thresh}}$ with the published
values. Purpose: verify that the extended pipeline reduces to the original
framework in the appropriate limit before scientific extensions are added.

\section{Injection Recovery}
\label{sec:injection_recovery}

\emph{Scaffold.}
Given injected values of $S_{\mathrm{thresh}}$, $x_{\mathrm{H_2O}}$, and
$f_{\mathrm{rgh}}$, the hypothesis-test pipeline must recover them within
their posterior credible intervals. Recovery tests over a small grid to
check for systematic biases before applying the pipeline to the sensitivity
studies of \Cref{chap:results}.
