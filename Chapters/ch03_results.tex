\section{HZIED Baseline: Turbet Steam Model}
\label{sec:hzied_plato_ch3}

With the observable sample established in \Cref{sec:planet_yield}, the
\HZIED hypothesis test is run on P4 and P5 using the \textcite{Turbet2020}
steam-atmosphere grid as the inflation prescription, combined with the
\textcite{DornLichtenberg2021} water-retention correction
($x_{\mathrm{H_2O}} = 0.005$, $f_{\mathrm{rgh}} = 0.8$).

The P4 sample ($N = 80$) yields $\ln\Delta Z = 26.2$, which exceeds the
strong-evidence threshold ($\ln\Delta Z > 5$) by a wide margin.  The
P5 sample ($N = 58$) yields $\ln\Delta Z = 10.3$, also a strong detection.
Both values are substantially lower than the generic reference
($\ln\Delta Z = 1114$) because the \PLATO samples are finite: the number of
detectable rocky habitable-zone planets is set by the PIC.

The depth cut of $80\,\mathrm{ppm}$ is conservative for \PLATO.
Relaxing it to $60\,\mathrm{ppm}$ (physically justified: an Earth-sized
planet around a solar-type star produces
$\delta = (R_p/R_\star)^2 \approx 84\,\mathrm{ppm}$) leaves P4 unchanged
($N = 80$, $\ln\Delta Z = 24.5$) but raises P5 from $N = 58$ to $N = 82$
($+41\,\%$) and its Bayes factor from $10.3$ to $44.3$.  All subsequent
analyses use the relaxed $60\,\mathrm{ppm}$ threshold as the fiducial.
